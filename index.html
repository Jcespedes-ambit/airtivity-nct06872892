01 — About the study

Understanding the AIRTIVITY™ Study

A Phase 3 study of an investigational tablet aimed at reducing flare-ups.

Participants are placed by chance into one of two groups — one takes the investigational tablet, the other takes a matching placebo that looks the same but contains no medicine. Neither the family nor the study team chooses the group.

Participants take one tablet a day for up to about a year and a half, log symptoms in a smartphone diary, and visit the study site up to about ten times. The study compares how many flare-ups each group has over roughly 20 months total.

02 — About Bronchiectasis

What AIRTIVITY™ Is Studying

Bronchiectasis is a chronic lung condition. These are the hallmark features the study is designed to address.

What it is

A chronic lung condition

The airways become widened and damaged over time, which makes it harder to clear mucus from the lungs.

Symptom

Ongoing cough and sputum

A long-lasting cough that brings up mucus (sputum) is one of the most common day-to-day symptoms.

Flare-ups

Exacerbations

Periods when symptoms suddenly get worse, sometimes needing antibiotics or other treatment.

The approach

Targeting inflammation

The investigational tablet is being studied for its potential to reduce the airway inflammation linked to flare-ups.

06 — FAQ

Common Questions

What is being studied in AIRTIVITY™?

The study evaluates an investigational once-daily tablet compared with a matching placebo, to see whether it helps reduce flare-ups in young people with bronchiectasis.

Who can take part?

The study is for adolescents aged 12 to 17 who have been diagnosed with bronchiectasis, produce sputum, and have had flare-ups. A study doctor confirms full eligibility.

How long does the study last?

Participants take one tablet a day for up to about a year and a half, and are in the study for roughly 20 months in total, including follow-up.

Who is sponsoring the study?

The study is sponsored by Boehringer Ingelheim. A study doctor at a participating site can answer detailed questions about taking part.
